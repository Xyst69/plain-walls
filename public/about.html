About The Studio

Stillness on paper.

Plain Walls was founded as an antidote to noisy home decor. We believe walls do not need high-frequency stimulation; they need balance, proportional breathing room, and subdued natural palettes that age gracefully with sunlight.

Why Thermal Lamination?

Traditional framed prints require fragile glass panes that reflect distracting glare and add unnecessary weight. We seal every print using a micro-thin thermal lamination film.

This treatment creates a wipe-clean surface impervious to ambient humidity, UV light, and fingerprints while preserving deep paper texture. Hang them directly with wooden hanger bars, clipboards, or inset them into glassless frames.

Dimensions Guide

A4 (21.0 × 29.7 cm) — Suitable for compact reading desks, vanity corners, and layered gallery shelf displays.
A3 (29.7 × 42.0 cm) — Balanced proportions for bedrooms, studio studies, and paired arrangements.
A2 (42.0 × 59.4 cm) — Command piece for dining credenzas, sofa walls, and high-ceiling hallways.
